How to play
  • Spawn phase: click on land to pick your start. You can re-pick until the timer ends.
  • Attack: click a tile → Attack. The slider sets what % of your troops you send. Attacks on neutral land are cheap; attacking players costs troops on both sides.
  • Boats: clicking land you don't border by land sends a transport ship across water (max 3 at once).
  • Troops grow toward your max (bigger territory + cities = higher max). Gold ticks in constantly; ports add more.
  • Build: City (+250k max troops), Port (gold, coast only), Defense Post (5x losses for attackers nearby), Missile Silo (launch nukes), SAM (shoots down nukes).
  • Alliances: click a player → Ally. Allies can't attack each other and can donate troops. Breaking one marks you as a traitor for 30s (weaker defense).
  • Win: own 80% of the land (configurable).
  • Keys: A attack hovered tile, B boat, 1–0 set attack %, Enter chat, Esc close menus, drag / wheel to pan & zoom.
