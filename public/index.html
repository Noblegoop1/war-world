WAR WORLD

Nation-scale warfare. Expand, build, research doctrines, wall your borders, field Mechs, nuke.

How to play

  • Spawn: click on land during the spawn phase (10s by default). Re-click to move until the timer ends.
  • Expand / attack: left-click any land you don't own. The slider (or Shift+scroll) sets how many troops you send. While you attack, your expansion leans toward your mouse. If the target isn't reachable by land, a boat is sent automatically. Crossed swords on the map show each attack's front and remaining troops.
  • Right-click opens the radial menu: build, boat, alliance, donate, declare war / make peace, info (the browser menu is disabled in-game).
  • Build with keys 1–0 or the bottom bar, then click a tile. Cities raise max troops, ports send trade ships, defense posts make attackers bleed and shell ships, silos launch nukes, SAMs shoot nukes down.
  • Factories 3 lay rail to every City/Port/Lab/Factory within 110 tiles and run trains that pay gold on arrival (more when the destination belongs to another nation). Upgrade a factory to level 2 to build Mechs; higher levels give tougher, longer-ranged Mechs.
  • Walls 8: click-and-drag on your land to draw a 3-tile-thick wall. It snaps to the coast and is built block by block (the slowest build). Enemies must grind it down and can't pass behind it. Price climbs steeply; nukes and mechs raze it.
  • Mechs 9: from a level-2 Factory. Click where it should patrol: it walks there and circles, shelling structures, mechs and land in range and stomping the ground — hit land turns neutral. Slow, land-locked and very tanky, but it bleeds HP inside enemy land. Click it, then a tile, to move it.
  • Artillery Battery & Repair Yard: a battery shells enemy Mechs and incoming attacks from 45 tiles away — it is the answer to a Mech on your border. A Repair Yard (needs the Field Engineering doctrine) heals your Mechs and rebuilds damaged walls nearby.
  • Airport & Airships: one Airport per nation, hugely expensive. Airships carry 5% of your troops over SAMs, warships and coastal guns and drop them inland — the way into a nation that has sealed every other route. SAMs can't shoot them — only a nation with Interceptor Screen (45% per pass, near its SAMs or Airport) or Airborne Mechs can bring one down. Your own SAMs can't sit within 70 tiles of your Airport.
  • Nukes kill population as well as land (up to 75% of the defender's army), and leave the ground irradiated for a couple of minutes. Irradiated ground is still free land — expensive to cross, but yours if you take it.
  • Selecting: click a unit or building — the hit area follows the icon, no zooming needed. Shift+drag selects several units at once; with a unit button armed the box only picks that kind.
  • Economy: Cities pay gold by level, land pays by its square root, and 90s without attacking a nation earns a ×1.3 peace dividend. Allies earn 75% more from trade ships sent to each other. Hover your gold to see where it comes from.
  • Declare war (radial on their land, or their nation card): your attacks on them carry 15% more troops, but your income drops 20% while the war lasts (at least 60s, then you can make peace).
  • Unit caps: 3 Mechs, 3 Warships, 2 Submarines, 3 Airships. Every level-3 Port adds a Warship and a Submarine to the cap, every level-3 Factory a Mech, and a level-4 building one more.
  • Info: right-click any building or unit (even an enemy's) → Info for its live stats. Upgrading a Factory or Port sends idle units home to refit to the new level.
  • Mech orders: right-click your own Mech for Hold / Roam border / Auto-defend / Auto-assault. A Mech also holds ground — attacks within 30 tiles lose 3x troops and move at half speed.
  • Defense Posts fortify the border stretch inside their radius (drawn in pale stone) and, if they are near the coast, shell passing enemy ships.
  • Warships 0: need a Port. Click water to set a patrol area; they hunt boats, trade ships and warships with shells. Click one, then water, to move it.
  • Research Lab 7: needs 3 Cities and a Factory within rail range. Pick 1 of 3 random doctrines (Mech, Navy, Air, Defense, Economy, Nuclear…). Some unlock units — submarines, naval mines, bomber strikes — which appear on the bar. Each lab is worth 2 doctrines and every extra lab costs 5x the last; research takes 3-5 minutes (faster in an upgraded lab) and is cancelled if you lose the lab. Cluster Munitions, Decoy Warheads, Hypersonic Missiles and SEAD are the answers to SAMs. Hide doctrines under the cards lets you look around the map before choosing.
  • Conquering a nation or tribe pays gold based on its size and treasury.
  • Click any nation to see its ATK POWER and ECONOMY — attack power drops while its forces are away fighting. Hover either number for the full breakdown.
  • A attack under cursor · B boat under cursor · R retaliate against your latest attacker · C center on you · N/H nukes · Enter chat · Esc close.
  • Win by owning 80% of the land (configurable). A player squeezed under 100 tiles is conquered outright.
